# Wallpaper generation

All three wallpapers were made with the built-in imagegen tool. The original was reconstructed from the supplied theme reference; the two landscape alternatives were generated as new illustrations. The files are stored in `theme/assets/` and are 1672 × 941 PNGs.

The focal palace is small and off center toward the left; preserve very quiet center and right-upper areas. Style: exquisite fine pale lavender ink linework and extremely soft watercolor on warm ivory paper, airy ethereal understated fantasy, in keeping with Re:Zero. Palette: warm ivory #f2efe7, pale lavender #d4cbe2, dusty lilac #b7a8cb, restrained mauve shadows; overall very pale, low contrast, never a dark night wallpaper. A few delicate lavender leaves or feathers can rest near the corners. Text: none. Constraints: wallpaper artwork only, no UI, no clock, no border, no typography, no logo, no watermark, no stars or sparkles, no foreground portrait, no large central objects.
